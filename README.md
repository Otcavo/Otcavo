## Research

My Ph.D. at the University of Maryland was on **computational geometry in the Funk and Hilbert metrics**. Highlights:

- 11 peer-reviewed papers at SoCG, SWAT, WADS and CCCG, and 7 talks at international conferences in 5 countries
- Solved the previously open distance and diameter problems on hyperbolic surfaces (arXiv, 2026)
- Figures for *Computational Intractability* (MIT Press, 2026) and mathematical art shown at the Bridges 2026 exhibition
